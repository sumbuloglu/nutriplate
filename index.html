      Which allergens should we check?

      This guide checks the listed ingredients, not product labels or kitchen cross-contact. No match does not mean a meal is safe for an allergy. Check every ingredient and replacement label, including “may contain” statements. Other foods can also cause allergies. Gluten-free does not always mean wheat-free; lactose-free milk still contains milk proteins.

      Sources & how alternatives work

      The 14 groups follow the Food Standards Agency allergen guide. Egg replacement guidance is informed by FARE egg substitutions. Other swaps are cooking ideas for these example recipes, not tested allergy-safe versions. Alternatives do not change the recipe or nutrition calculation; those still describe the original ingredients.

      Educational estimates, not laboratory measurements. Brands, recipes and cooking methods change nutrition values. This calculator estimates meal composition; it does not prescribe calorie targets.

      About the numbers & ingredient reference

      NutriPlate uses a built-in collection of 50 original example recipes and rounded, illustrative food-composition values per 100 g. These values are not a live database feed or a verified extract of specific USDA records. For precise tracking, use your product label or the matching food record in USDA FoodData Central.

      For each ingredient: nutrient contribution = weight in grams ÷ 100 × value per 100 g. We add those contributions, then multiply by the number of portions eaten. Recipe ingredients are listed in the state used for weighing: cooked rice means cooked weight, for example. Added water contributes no calories; evaporation changes weight but is not modeled here. All listed oil is included in the calculation.

      The energy bars use the general 4/4/9 factors described by the USDA Food and Nutrition Information Center. Listed food calories can differ from that approximation because of fiber, rounding and food-specific factors. Salt and optional seasonings are not included; sodium, vitamins and minerals are not calculated.
